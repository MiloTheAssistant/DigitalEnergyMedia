# Digital Energy Media Brand Photo Assets Design

## Goal

Create a project-ready image library for Digital Energy Media that complements the existing abstract hero image and gives the brand a more human, recognizable profile stamp.

## Direction

The approved direction is **Hybrid Brand Library** with a **Local Growth System** vibe. Assets should feel useful to small and mid-sized businesses: practical, local, professional, warm, and modern. The visual language should borrow the existing hero's dark contrast, cyan/amber energy, and AI visibility cues, but it should be more human and less purely abstract.

## Logo/Profile Stamp

The main profile image should be a human-focused brand logo/profile stamp, not another wide hero background. It should work as the primary social profile picture for Digital Energy Media.

The stamp should avoid fake readable text and avoid detailed small typography. It should use an emblem-like composition that can survive circular cropping: a human/operator signal, local business visibility, and digital energy/AI media cues. It can imply the initials or mark structure for Digital Energy Media, but it should not rely on generated words being legible.

## Background Asset Library

The supporting social backgrounds should be clean photoreal backgrounds with no embedded text or logos. They should leave usable negative space for future platform-specific copy, logos, or campaign overlays.

## Quality Rules

- Photorealistic backgrounds should look premium and plausible, not stock-like.
- No readable AI-generated text, slogans, watermarks, fake logos, or brand names inside the image.
- Keep people anonymous or backgrounded in supporting backgrounds; the profile stamp can be human-centered but should read as a brand mark rather than a portrait.
- Use dark, clean contrast with cyan/amber highlights that harmonize with the existing hero.
- Preserve safe negative space for overlays.
- Avoid cluttered interfaces, warped devices, distorted hands, and illegible UI panels.
- Save final assets in the project under `public/brand`, not only in Codex-generated output folders.

## Verification

After generation, confirm each planned file exists, has nonzero size, and can be opened as an image. Prefer PNG outputs for broad compatibility with the current project.
